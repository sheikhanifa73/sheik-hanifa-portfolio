From a conversation
to a release.

My proposed delivery framework for a new project. Scope, estimates and release decisions are agreed with the team and client.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the business

Marketing handover, stakeholder call, user journeys, requirements, constraints and measurable acceptance criteria.

02 / VALIDATE

Check feasibility

API access, integrations, data privacy, offline needs and technical spikes. Record risks and agree the scope.

03 / DESIGN

Prototype together

Collaborate with design in Figma or Adobe XD. Review flows, accessibility and edge cases before client approval.

04 / PLAN

Make delivery visible

Agree milestones. Break work into Jira stories with acceptance criteria, estimates, owners and dependencies.

05 / BUILD

Ship in small increments

Implement, review and test each change. Share a weekly demo video with completed work, risks and next steps.

06 / RELEASE

Validate and support

UAT, signing, TestFlight and Play testing, privacy disclosures, rollout approval, crash monitoring and maintenance.
